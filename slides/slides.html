Graph-Based Smart Contract Auditing

A graph attention network prototype, tested by leave-one-contract-out and on new contracts
Caleb Ablorh-Adjei · Student ID 22186668 · October 2026
Motivation

Deployed contracts cannot be patched

  • Smart contracts hold real assets on Ethereum; a flaw is exploitable as soon as it is deployed.
  • Three classes studied: Reentrancy, Timestamp dependency, Arithmetic error.
  • Existing tools disagree with each other and flag many real contracts (Durieux et al., 2020).
  • Idea: treat a contract as a graph of control flow and data flow, and learn over it.
2
Research questions

What this project asks

  • RQ1. Can a GAT over a sliced contract graph separate vulnerable from safe contracts?
  • RQ2. Does it generalize to contracts it has not seen, compared with simple baselines?
  • RQ3. Can its node scores point to the vulnerable line?
We report a feasibility result, not a detection rate.

Two protocols

Held out, and new contracts

Leave-one-contract-out

  • Six demo contracts; hold one out, train on five.
  • 18 decisions per method, 3 positive.
  • GAT over 5 seeds; baselines: sink-presence rule, logistic regression.

External test

  • Train on all six; score six new contracts (different style; one solc 0.7.6).
  • 18 decisions, 3 positive.
  • Caveat: one contract informed the arithmetic fix, so this is not independent.

Error analysis

Why each error happens

  • GAT: errors repeat in every seed. Held-out Reentrancy: the only training example is the safe contract, which scores the pattern as safe. A held-out safe contract scores 0.96 for Reentrancy.
  • Sink rule, keywords: send matches inside msg.sender and "Failed to send", so Arithmetic contracts are flagged for Reentrancy.
  • Sink rule, after the fix: any +=, -= or * is an arithmetic sink, so six false positives.
10
Localization

Lines: in-sample yes, held out no

  • In-sample, Reentrancy is localized to lines 15 and 16 (external call and its check).
  • In-sample, Timestamp is localized to line 9, after a label fix.
  • Arithmetic is flagged at 99.88% but not localized.
  • Held out: localization fails in all 15 vulnerable-contract evaluations.
  • External: vault exact (14–15); raffle 13 plus one extra line; ledger not localized.

Defects found and fixed

Found while checking the work

  • Wrong training labels for two contracts (lines 10 and 14, instead of 9 and 12).
  • Legacy-AST operator lookup failed for solc 0.7.6, so the arithmetic contract had no sink.
  • Compound assignments ignored by the arithmetic check. Found on the new contract; fixed; it weakened demo-set separation.
  • Unverifiable references removed; the rest checked against publisher or arXiv pages.
Pre-fix logs are kept in results/logs/before_fixes/ and results/logs/before_arithmetic_fix/. Full list: FAILURE_LOG.md.
12
Limitations

What this does not show

  • Six training and six external contracts, all written by the author: not real code.
  • The external test informed a fix, so it is optimistic.
  • Keyword features match substrings (now, send).
  • Thresholds set by hand; compiler versions 0.4–0.6 untested.
  • Public benchmarks (SmartBugs, SolidiFI) not yet used.

What would make a detection claim possible
